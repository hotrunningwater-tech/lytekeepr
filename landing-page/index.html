What LyteKeepr does for you

LyteKeepr continuously watches your B2B relationships across LinkedIn, email, and your CRM and tells you what needs attention, why, and what to do next.

Example: Sarah Chen — Follow-up ready

You sent the pricing information Sarah requested six days ago. She has not replied on LinkedIn or email.

See Suggested Follow-up

Channel: LinkedIn

Suggested reply:

"Hi Sarah — just following up on the pricing details I sent over last week. Let me know if anything needs clarifying, or if it'd help to hop on a quick call this week."

LyteKeepr recommends following up now and prepares the reply for you.

Example: Michael Torres — New reply

Michael replied by email this morning with a question about your security review.

See Suggested Reply

Channel: Email

Suggested reply:

"Hi Michael — thanks for the question. Here's a quick summary of our security review process, along with the documentation you asked about. Happy to walk your team through it if that's useful."

LyteKeepr understands the previous conversation and prepares the appropriate response.

Example: Priya Shah — No action needed

You emailed Priya two days ago, but she replied on LinkedIn yesterday saying she would review the proposal with her team.

When Do You Plan to Follow Up?

LyteKeepr will keep watching this one and let you know if Priya hasn't followed up with her team in a few days.

LyteKeepr connects both channels and knows no follow-up is needed yet.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is LyteKeepr?

LyteKeepr is an AI copilot that follows your B2B relationships across LinkedIn and email, uses your CRM context, knows when something needs attention, and prepares the next response.

Do I need to set follow-up reminders?

No. LyteKeepr keeps track of the relationship and decides when another action makes sense.

Does it prepare the message too?

Yes. It prepares the email or LinkedIn reply using the relevant relationship history.

What if someone replies on another channel?

LyteKeepr connects LinkedIn and email activity into the same relationship and updates the status accordingly.

Can I ask LyteKeepr questions?

Yes. You can ask what happened, what somebody said, what you promised, why a follow-up is recommended, or ask it to draft or adjust a response.

Does it work with my existing CRM?

Yes. LyteKeepr works alongside the CRM you already use.

Do I control what gets sent?

Yes. Nothing is sent without your approval.
